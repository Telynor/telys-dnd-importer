Copperwork Watcher
Medium construct, unaligned
Armor Class 16 (copper plating)
Hit Points 45 (6d8 + 18)
Speed 30 ft., climb 20 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
16 (+3) 12 (+1) 16 (+3) 8 (-1) 14 (+2) 6 (-2)
Saving Throws Con +5, Wis +4
Skills Perception +4
Damage Immunities poison
Condition Immunities poisoned
Senses darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14
Languages Common
Challenge 3 (700 XP)
Proficiency Bonus +2

Sealed Chassis. The watcher can breathe air and water.

Actions
Multiattack. The watcher makes three attacks: two Claw attacks and one Spark Bolt attack.
Claw. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.
Spark Bolt. Ranged Spell Attack: +4 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (2d6) lightning damage. The target must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be blinded for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.
Steam Cone (Recharge 5-6). The watcher exhales steam in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
