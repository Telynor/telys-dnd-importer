Clockwarden
Hit Dice: 1d8 per clockwarden level
Armor: Light armor, shields
Weapons: Simple weapons
Saving Throws: Intelligence, Constitution
Skills: Choose two from Arcana, History, Investigation, and Perception
Spellcasting Progression: none

Class Features

Level 1: Borrowed Moment
As a bonus action, you gain 1d4 temporary hit points. You can use this feature 3 times before finishing a long rest.

Level 2: Pendulum Strike
As an action, make a melee spell attack against one creature. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 force damage.

Level 4: Ability Score Improvement
Increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. You cannot increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.
